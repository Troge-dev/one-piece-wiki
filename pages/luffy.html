Who is Luffy?

Monkey D. Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates. He is a cheerful, adventurous, and determined pirate who dreams of becoming the King of the Pirates.

History

Luffy grew up in Foosha Village in the East Blue. Inspired by the pirate Red-Haired Shanks, he began his journey to become a pirate. He gathered his crew and sailed across the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as One Piece.

Personal Goal

Luffy's greatest dream is to become the King of the Pirates by finding the One Piece and achieving the freedom he has always wanted.
