Who is Usopp?

Usopp is the sniper of the Straw Hat Pirates. He is creative, clever, and skilled at using different weapons. Although he can be afraid during dangerous situations, he continues to support and protect his friends.

History

Usopp grew up in Syrup Village with his mother, Banchina. His father, Yasopp, left to become a pirate. Usopp became friends with Kaya and often told stories about imaginary adventures. He later joined Luffy and the Straw Hat Pirates, beginning his journey across the seas.

Personal Goal

Usopp dreams of becoming a brave warrior of the sea. He wants to overcome his fears and become someone who can face any danger with courage.
