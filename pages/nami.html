Who is Nami?

Nami is the navigator of the Straw Hat Pirates. She is intelligent, clever, and skilled at reading weather conditions and navigating the seas. She also loves money and dreams of drawing a complete map of the world.

History

Nami was raised in Cocoyasi Village by Bell-mère alongside her sister Nojiko. She became a skilled cartographer and navigator. Before joining Luffy's crew, she worked under the Arlong Pirates because she wanted to free her village. Luffy and his friends later helped her defeat Arlong and protect her home.

Personal Goal

Nami's dream is to draw a complete map of the entire world. She wants to travel across every sea and explore different islands to complete her dream.
