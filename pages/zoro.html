Who is Zoro?

Roronoa Zoro is the swordsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of Luffy's earliest crew members. He is known for his strength, determination, and fighting style using three swords. He is also one of the crew's strongest fighters.

History

Zoro trained in swordsmanship from a young age and made a promise to his childhood friend Kuina to become the world's greatest swordsman. After Kuina's death, he continued training with her sword. He later met Luffy and joined the Straw Hat Pirates, beginning his journey across the seas.

Personal Goal

Zoro's dream is to become the world's greatest swordsman. He wants to fulfill the promise he made to Kuina and prove himself as the strongest swordsman.
