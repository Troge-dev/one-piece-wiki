Who is Going Merry?

The Going Merry is the first official ship of the Straw Hat Pirates, gifted to Luffy and his crew by Kaya after the events in Syrup Village. Designed as a caravel-class ship by Kaya's steward Merry, she carried the crew through the entire East Blue and across the first half of the Grand Line—withstanding battles, scaling Reverse Mountain, and flying to Skypiea. Deeply loved by her crew, the Merry famously developed a sentient ship spirit called a Klabautermann, allowing her to sail one last time to rescue the Straw Hats at Enies Lobby before receiving a legendary viking funeral.

History

Gifted to the Straw Hat Pirates by Kaya after the events in Syrup Village, the caravel-class Going Merry served as the crew's loyal floating home through the East Blue and across the first half of the Grand Line. Despite sustaining heavy structural damage that eventually forced her retirement in Water 7, the Merry's deep bond with her crew manifested a living ship spirit called a Klabautermann, which miraculously enabled her to sail one last time to rescue them at Enies Lobby before receiving a heartbreaking and legendary viking funeral.

Personal Goal

To faithfully carry her crew across every sea and serve as their beloved floating home until every member achieved their dreams.
