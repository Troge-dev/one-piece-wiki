Who is Jinbe?

"Knight of the Sea" Jinbe is the helmsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of the senior officers of the Straw Hat Grand Fleet, serving as the tenth member of the crew. He is a whale shark fish-man and a powerful master of Fish-Man Karate whose dream is to fulfill his former captain Fisher Tiger's dying wish of coexistence and equality between humans and fish-men. Having previously served as the captain of the Sun Pirates and as one of the Seven Warlords of the Sea, Jinbe befriended Monkey D. Luffy during the Summit War of Marineford and later officially joined the Straw Hat Pirates during the Wano Country Arc.

History

"Knight of the Sea" Jinbe is a whale shark fish-man and the helmsman of the Straw Hat Pirates. Originally serving as a soldier in the Neptune Army, he joined Fisher Tiger and the Sun Pirates, eventually becoming the crew's second captain after Tiger's death. To protect Fish-Man Island, Jinbe later became one of the Seven Warlords of the Sea, but he resigned during the Summit War of Marineford after befriending Monkey D. Luffy. After aiding Luffy and severing ties with Big Mom, Jinbe officially joined the Straw Hat Pirates during the Wano Country Arc to help fulfill his dream of true equality and coexistence between humans and fish-men.

Personal Goal

Jinbe's personal goal is to fulfill his former captain Fisher Tiger's dying wish of achieving true equality and peaceful coexistence between humans and fish-men.
