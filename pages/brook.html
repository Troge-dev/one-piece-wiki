Who is Brook?

"Soul King" Brook is the musician and swordsman of the Straw Hat Pirates. Originally the captain of the Esperia Kingdom's Battle Convoy and a member of the Rumbar Pirates, he was resurrected by the Yomi Yomi no Mi, though his body was reduced to a skeleton during the process. After drifting in the Florian Triangle for 50 years, he met Luffy and officially joined the crew to fulfill his dream of reuniting with his old whale friend, Laboon, at Reverse Mountain.

History

Born 90 years ago in the Esperia Kingdom of the West Blue, Brook survived a harsh childhood before being rescued and raised by Prince Reuven, eventually rising to become captain of the kingdom's Battle Convoy, a skilled swordsman, and a musician who trained young Princess Shuri. Tragedy struck when a mysterious toxic mist devastated the kingdom and forced Reuven into a doomed war against the World Government; during the final assault, Brook suffered the devastating heartbreak of discovering that Shuri was a World Noble who had betrayed and slain her adoptive father.

Personal Goal

Brook's ultimate personal goal is to fulfill his decades-old promise to Laboon, a giant island whale waiting at the reverse mountain entrance of the Grand Line.
