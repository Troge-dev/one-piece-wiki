Who is Thousand Sunny?

The Thousand Sunny is the second and current primary ship of the Straw Hat Pirates, succeeding the Going Merry. Built in Water 7 by Franky using rare and indestructible Adam Wood, she is a magnificent brigantine-class ship designed to sail across the entire world. Named by Iceburg to signify a ship that will proudly sail through thousands of rough seas, she features incredible innovations like the Soldier Dock System, an aquarium bar, and the emergency Coup de Burst rocket propulsion system.

History

Conceived in Water 7 after the heartbreaking loss of the Going Merry, the Thousand Sunny was masterfully designed and built by the shipwright Franky using rare, indestructible Adam Wood, with iceburg officially naming the vessel to symbolize a ship that would cheerfully brave thousands of rough seas. Since its grand completion and maiden voyage, this magnificent brigantine has served as the indomitable floating home for the Straw Hat Pirates, utilizing its signature Coup de Burst rocket propulsion and innovative facilities to carry the entire crew safely through the trials of the Grand Line and deep into the treacherous waters of the New World.

Personal Goal

The personal goal of the Thousand Sunny is to cheerfully brave thousands of rough seas and carry the entire Straw Hat crew all the way to the end of the Grand Line so every member can achieve their dreams.
